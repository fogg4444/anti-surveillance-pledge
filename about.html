How the pledge works

We send the same request to every sitting council member and every declared council candidate in the county. We give a deadline. We publish what comes back, in full, with the date we asked.

If someone signs, they go on the record as a signer. If they respond with a different position, we print their response as they wrote it. If they don't respond before the deadline, we note that.

We don't edit responses, and we don't characterize a position the person didn't take. If we get something wrong, write to us and we'll correct it.

What signing does not mean

Signing the pledge is not an endorsement of a candidate by anyone involved in this project, and it isn't a statement about any other issue. Candidates from any party are welcome to sign.
